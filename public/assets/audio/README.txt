Integrated game audio lives here.

Folders:
- ui: short interface sounds and extracted UI variations
- wheel: wheel start/spin/tick sounds
- rewards: win, lose, prize, and jackpot sounds
- gameplay: impacts, slices, heavy hits
- transitions: whooshes, pops, pickups
- rhythm: rhythm and dance feedback
- movement: jump, bounce, dash sounds
- music: menu, gameplay, result, and danger tracks

Drop new raw files into public/assets/audio_inbox first.
